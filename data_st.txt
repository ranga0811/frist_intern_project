Once upon a time, there was a beautiful village surrounded by green mountains.
A young boy lived in that village with his family.
Every morning, the boy walked through the forest and enjoyed the beautiful nature.
One day, he found a small bird that was unable to fly.
He carefully helped the bird and took care of it.
After a few days, the bird became healthy and flew back into the beautiful sky.
The boy was very happy because he had helped a small creature.
